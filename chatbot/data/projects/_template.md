# Project Name

> One-sentence positioning: what this is and who it's for.

## Overview
One paragraph: the problem, the solution, the outcome.

## Why
The business / scientific / engineering problem. What was at stake. What the
alternative was. Why this mattered enough to build.

## What
Scope — what was built. Explicit non-goals — what was left out and why.

## How — Architecture
High-level architecture: components, data flow, key dependencies. A diagram
description if you have one.

## How — Tech Stack
Languages, frameworks, infrastructure, models, observability tooling.

## Journey: POC
Initial hypothesis. Scope of the proof-of-concept. What was learned. What was
wrong and what changed because of it.

## Journey: MVP
What got hardened from POC. New requirements that emerged. Decisions made
under pressure.

## Journey: Production
Reliability, observability, scale targets, security / regulatory posture,
on-call patterns.

## Key Decisions & Tradeoffs
The decisions worth writing down: chose X over Y because Z. What we'd revisit.

## Outcomes
Verified metrics. Business / user impact. Lessons.
